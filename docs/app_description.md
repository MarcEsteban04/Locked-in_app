# Locked In
### Your Personal AI Study Companion

> **Turn your lectures into personalized reviewers, quizzes, mock exams, and study plans—all powered by AI.**

---

# 📖 Overview

**Locked In** is an AI-powered study companion designed for **high school and college students**. Instead of spending hours rewriting notes and creating reviewers manually, students simply upload their lecture materials, and Locked In transforms them into interactive learning experiences.

Whether it's a PDF, PowerPoint, handwritten notes, lecture recording, or even a picture of the classroom whiteboard, **Locked In** automatically generates:

- AI-powered reviewers
- Smart summaries
- Flashcards
- Practice quizzes
- Mock exams
- Personalized study plans
- Learning analytics
- Weakness detection

The goal isn't just to help students study harder—it helps them **study smarter.**
